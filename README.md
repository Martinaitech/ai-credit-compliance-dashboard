# AI-Assisted Credit Decisioning & Compliance Analytics System

## Business Problem

A simulated Jamaican financial institution implemented AI-assisted credit scoring but lacked oversight visibility into KYC exposure, operational bottlenecks, and algorithmic governance risks.

## Key Findings

### 1. 65% Peak KYC Risk Exposure
The Result: Onboarding failure flags peaked between 58% and 65% across specific parishes.

The Meaning: Indicates broken onboarding controls, weak verification pipelines, and elevated compliance exposure under JDPA/OIC governance standards.

### 2. 48-Hour Operational Bottlenecks
The Result: Average automated decision processing plateaued near 48 hours.

The Meaning: Revealed escalation overload into manual review queues, reducing automation efficiency and increasing operational cost.

### 3. 60% Algorithmic Approval Ceiling
The Result: Approval rates remained capped near 60% across multiple geographic regions.

The Meaning: Suggests restrictive decision thresholds or possible algorithmic filtering patterns requiring governance review.

## Solution Delivered

Built a governance-focused MIS oversight dashboard providing:
- KYC risk visibility
- Operational monitoring
- Executive-level compliance reporting
- AI-assisted decision oversight analytics
